About TeqStudy

TeqStudy is a free educational site focused on practical software development, embedded systems, networking, game development, cybersecurity, and related technical skills.

The goal is simple: give learners clear, structured paths they can actually finish — with lessons, quizzes, code examples, labs, and capstone projects — without accounts, paywalls, or scattered tutorials.

What you’ll find here

  • Full developer tracks (Java, Python, DevOps, Unity, Embedded Systems & IoT, and more)
  • Self-contained course pages that work offline once loaded
  • Progress saved locally in your browser
  • Honest framing — especially in areas like trading education where risk disclosure matters

How it’s built

The entire site is static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. There is no backend user system. Course progress lives in your browser’s localStorage so you can leave and return without creating an account.

Who it’s for

Self-taught developers, career switchers, students preparing for technical roles, and anyone who wants a coherent path instead of a pile of bookmarks. Courses range from complete-beginner friendly to intermediate and job-oriented.

If a course helps you, the best thing you can do is finish it and build something with what you learned.
