Software Design & Principles - Full Course Summary

This course includes 14 weeks of complete lesson notes, labs, assignments, quizzes, capstone, and instructor guide.

Course Motto:
Good software is not just software that works. Good software is software that can be understood, changed, tested, and trusted.

Weekly Topics:
Week 1: What Software Design Really Means
Week 2: Requirements, Constraints, and Acceptance Criteria
Week 3: Decomposition, Modularity, Cohesion, and Coupling
Week 4: Object-Oriented Design That Does Not Fight You
Week 5: SOLID Part 1: SRP, OCP, and LSP
Week 6: SOLID Part 2: Interfaces, Dependency Inversion, and Testability
Week 7: DRY, KISS, YAGNI, and Naming
Week 8: UML and Practical Design Diagrams
Week 9: Design Patterns Part 1: Strategy, Factory, Observer, Singleton
Week 10: Design Patterns Part 2: Adapter, Decorator, Command, Repository, MVC/MVVM
Week 11: Testing and Testable Design
Week 12: Software Architecture Basics
Week 13: Refactoring, Code Smells, and Technical Debt
Week 14: Final Design Review and Professional Presentation
