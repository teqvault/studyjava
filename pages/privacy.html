Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

TeqStudy (teqvault.study) is a free educational website that provides developer learning courses and study materials. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit the site.

Information We Collect

  • Automatically collected data: Standard server and analytics information such as IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and referring URLs. This comes from normal web server logs and any analytics tools in use.
  • Local storage: Many courses save progress (completed lessons, quiz results) in your browser’s localStorage so your place is remembered between visits. This data stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.
  • No accounts required: We do not require user accounts and do not collect names, emails, or passwords for course access.

Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized ads based on your browsing activity. You can learn more about how Google uses data in its Privacy Policy and manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.

This site publishes a standard ads.txt file authorizing Google as a direct seller of advertising inventory.

How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the website
  • To understand which courses and pages are useful
  • To display relevant advertisements
  • To maintain security and diagnose technical issues

Third-Party Services

This site may load resources from third parties (Google Fonts, Google AdSense, and any analytics providers). These services have their own privacy policies and may collect data independently of TeqStudy.

Data Retention

Server logs are retained only as long as needed for security and operational purposes. Progress data stored in your browser remains until you clear it through browser settings.

Children’s Privacy

This site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

  • You can clear localStorage at any time in your browser settings.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads through Google’s ad settings.
  • You can use browser tools that block cookies and tracking.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the most recent version.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to the address listed on the Contact page.
